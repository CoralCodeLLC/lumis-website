How it works

Syllabus week, solved in one sitting

The syllabus already contains your entire semester. Lumis is the first planner that treats it that way — and then keeps learning your course all term.

Drop your syllabi

Snap a photo or upload the PDF or DOCX for every course — or send it straight from Files, Mail, or Canvas with the share sheet.

Lumis reads every line

Every assignment, quiz, exam, reading and grading weight comes out — even the schedule buried in a table on page six.

Add your course material

Upload lecture notes, slide decks, readings and your own notes. Lumis learns your course from what your professor actually teaches.

Your semester appears

One calendar, per-course trackers, smart reminders, flashcards, quizzes and a tutor that knows your material — all generated for you.

Our story

Two students at the University of Miami who wanted one app for all of it

We're Hudson and Jesse — students at the University of Miami and friends who kept having the same conversation every August: why does surviving a semester take five different apps? A calendar for deadlines, a flashcard app for studying, a spreadsheet for grades, a notes app, a timer — and a syllabus buried in PDFs that none of them ever read.

So we built the tool we actually wanted: everything you need for the semester, in one place. Upload your syllabi once and Lumis becomes your calendar, your assignment tracker, your flashcards and quizzes, your GPA calculator, your study timer, and your tutor — one app that knows your whole course.

We're not an ed-tech giant. We're two students who upload our own syllabi every semester and test every feature on our own classes first. If it doesn't survive a Miami finals week, it doesn't ship.

Lumis is built by Coral Code, the two-person studio we started to make the software we wished existed. Lumis is our first product, and it's just the beginning.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else — email hello@lumisapp.org.

When does Lumis launch?

Beta testing on TestFlight starts in August 2026, with the App Store launch targeted for the start of the school year. Waitlist members get beta invites first.

What can I upload besides the syllabus?

Lecture notes, slide decks, readings, handouts, lab manuals, past problem sets, and your own notes — as PDFs, documents, or photos. Everything you add goes into that course's knowledge base, so its flashcards, quizzes and explanations get sharper as the semester goes on.

What does "Lumis researches my course" mean?

Your syllabus names the course and its topics but rarely explains them. Lumis takes that topic list and builds its own background on each one — the standard concepts, key terms and worked examples a course like yours covers — so it can teach a week that the syllabus describes in four words. Your material always comes first; research fills the gaps around it.

Will it read my professor's messy syllabus?

That's the whole point. Lumis handles PDFs, DOCX files and photos, and it's built for real syllabi — schedules buried in tables, dates without years, "week 9" formats, and recurring quizzes. If it ever misses one, you can fix it in a tap.

What happens to the files I upload?

They're processed to build your personal semester plan and study materials for your account — that's it. Your courses, notes and uploads belong to you, we don't sell data, and you can delete any file, any course, or your whole account at any time. Details in our privacy policy.

Is using it allowed at my school?

Lumis is a study tool, not a ghostwriter — it organizes your semester and quizzes you on your own material. Academic-integrity rules vary by course and instructor, so follow your school's policy on AI assistance for graded work.

What platforms is it on?

iPhone first, including the home-screen widget and share-sheet import. Other platforms come later — the waitlist is where we'll announce them.

How much will Premium cost?

Final pricing is announced at launch, priced for a student budget, with a 7-day free trial. Joining the waitlist locks in the early-bird rate.
