How it works

Syllabus week, solved in one sitting

The syllabus already contains your entire semester. Lumis is the first planner that treats it that way.

Drop your syllabi

Snap a photo or upload the PDF or DOCX for each course — all of them, right during syllabus week.

Lumis reads every line

AI extracts each assignment, quiz, exam, reading, and grading weight — even the schedule buried in a table on page six.

Your semester appears

A unified calendar, per-course trackers, smart reminders, and study tools generated from your real course material.

Features

A planner that also studies with you

Planners know your deadlines but can't teach. Flashcard apps can quiz you but don't know your semester. Lumis does both — from one syllabus upload.

Syllabus scanner

Photo or file in — every dated deadline and grade weight out. No more typing your semester in by hand.

Semester calendar

Every course on one calendar with an all-assignments view — and a home-screen widget so today's work finds you.

Grade-weighted priorities

A 30% final outranks a 2% quiz. Lumis knows what each assignment is worth and plans your effort accordingly.

AI flashcards & quizzes

Decks and practice quizzes generated from your actual course topics and uploaded notes — not someone else's deck.

Focus & study timer

Per-class study sessions, a lock-in Focus tab, and a finals mode for when it's time to buckle down.

GPA tracking

A live GPA calculator with predictions — see what you need on the final before the final.

Our story

Built by two students who were tired of syllabus week

Every semester starts the same way: five PDFs, each hiding a different table of deadlines, and an evening lost to copying them into a calendar by hand — hoping you didn't miss the quiz on page six.

We built Lumis because the syllabus already is your semester. Someone just had to treat it that way. One upload should produce your calendar, your priorities, and your study materials — and then keep you honest through finals.

Lumis is an independent app, built close to the problem: we're students, we upload our own syllabi, and we test every feature on our own coursework first. No ed-tech giant, no data resale — just a tool we wanted badly enough to build.

Lumis is made by Coral Code, the two-person software company we founded to build products we wish existed. Lumis is our flagship.

When does Lumis launch?

Beta testing on TestFlight starts in August 2026, with the App Store launch targeted for the start of the school year. Waitlist members get beta invites first.

What platforms is it on?

iPhone first, including a home-screen widget and share-sheet import. Other platforms come later — the waitlist is where we'll announce them.

Will it read my professor's messy syllabus?

That's the whole point. Lumis handles PDFs, DOCX files, and photos, and it's built for real syllabi — schedules buried in tables, dates without years, "week 9" formats, and recurring quizzes. If it ever misses one, you can fix it in a tap.

What happens to my syllabus after I upload it?

It's processed to build your personal semester plan and study materials — that's it. Your courses and notes belong to you, we don't sell data, and you can delete your account and data anytime. Details in our privacy policy.

How much will Premium cost?

Final pricing is announced at launch, priced for a student budget with a 7-day free trial. Joining the waitlist locks in the early-bird rate.
